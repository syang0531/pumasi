# Pumasi 1.19.0

High availability and the remote API. The API and the CLI read from
followers, submitters included; failed sign-in blocks and shared limits
carry over to a new leader; a replica restarted on another address
rejoins by itself; `?wait=` and `logs -f` are pushed by the control
plane; an account can cap the CPUs, memory and CU of each job; and
`pumasi ui --acme DOMAIN` gets and renews its own certificate from
Let's Encrypt.

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/syang0531/pumasi/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

On Windows, from an administrator PowerShell:
`irm https://github.com/syang0531/pumasi/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex`.
The agent protocol stays 14 (minor 10) and the stored state keeps its
layout (7). `pumasi upgrade latest` upgrades the agents and then the
control plane as before. Clients and dashboards of 1.19 work with a 1.18
control plane (they poll and read from the leader as before); caps per
job need every replica on 1.19, and `pumasi quota --max-job-*` or a
`cluster.toml` with `max_job_*` says so against an older one.

## New

- **Reads from followers for the API and the CLI.** `pumasi status`,
  `job`, `events`, `top` and the API's GET endpoints for the status,
  jobs, task details, events and metrics (history too) are answered by a
  follower when the ticket lists replicas, from the copy the leader sends
  every 2 seconds and only while it is fresher than 6 seconds. Jobs that
  are queued or running, and those that ended in the last ten minutes,
  come from the copy; older jobs and task output from the leader.
  Submitters read their own jobs and their capacity from followers too.
- **Limits that survive a failover.** The leader writes the shared
  request limits and the failed sign-in counts and blocks to the
  replicated state as one compacted record every 2 seconds when they
  changed, so the new leader keeps a block that was in place, and the
  failures counted toward one, when the old leader dies.
- **Replicas that move.** A replica restarted on another address (a new
  IP, another port) signs its new address with its key and the leader
  puts it in the membership, so it catches up again without `ha remove`
  and a new invite.
- **Pushed waits.** `?wait=` on a job and the new `?wait=` on a task's
  logs, `pumasi run` and `pumasi logs -f` are answered the moment the job
  ends or output arrives (within 100 ms in CI), not at the next poll.
- **Caps per job.** Besides quotas on the whole account:

  ```sh
  pumasi quota alice --max-job-cpus 8 --max-job-memory 32G --max-job-cu 20
  ```

  or `max_job_cpus`, `max_job_memory`, `max_job_cu` under
  `[accounts.alice]` in `cluster.toml`, the dashboard's account page, or
  `max_job_*` in the API's policy PATCH. A task asking for more CPUs or
  memory is refused at submission; a job that has spent its CU cap fails
  its tasks that have not run (event `job.cu_cap`) and lets the running
  ones finish. The caps bind every job of the account, whoever submits
  it.
- **ACME in `pumasi ui`.**

  ```sh
  pumasi ui --api-only --listen 0.0.0.0:443 --acme pumasi.example.com --acme-email ops@example.com
  ```

  TLS-ALPN-01 on the dashboard's own port by default, or
  `--acme-challenge http-01` with a listener on port 80
  (`--acme-http-listen`) that also sends browsers to HTTPS. The account
  key and certificate are kept in the home (`acme/`), renewed 30 days
  before they expire and swapped in without a restart.
  `--acme-directory letsencrypt-staging`, or any ACME URL with
  `--acme-ca FILE` for a private CA such as step-ca.

## Changed

- The 1.12 follower reads now include submitters: a follower answers a
  submitter's status once it has the account's capacity, with only that
  account's jobs.

## Known limits

- A crash can lose up to the last 2 seconds of limit counts; at most
  2,000 entries of each kind are kept (blocks first).
- A job can go over its CU cap by the runs under way when it reached it.
- ACME has no DNS-01 (no wildcard certificates) and no external account
  binding; the Pebble check runs on Linux CI only.
- A restarted replica on a new address may start one election before the
  leader has its address. Tickets made before still list the old
  address; make new ones for clients that reach the cluster through it.
- "Cloud sessions keep working through the tunnel while the owner
  restarts the leader" is checked by hand.

## 한국어 요약

- **API·CLI도 팔로워에서 읽습니다.** `pumasi status`·`job`·`events`·`top`과 API의 상태·작업·태스크 상세·이벤트·지표(기록 포함) 조회를, 티켓에 복제본이 있으면 팔로워가 리더가 2초마다 보내는 사본(6초보다 새로울 때만)으로 답합니다. 대기·실행 중이거나 10분 안에 끝난 작업은 사본에서, 그보다 오래된 작업과 태스크 출력은 리더에서 옵니다. submitter도 자기 작업과 capacity를 팔로워에서 읽습니다.
- **장애 전환에도 남는 제한.** 리더가 공유 요청 제한과 로그인 실패 횟수·차단을 바뀌었을 때 2초마다 압축한 기록 하나로 복제 상태에 씁니다. 리더가 죽어도 새 리더가 차단과 누적 실패를 이어 갑니다.
- **주소가 바뀐 복제본.** 다른 주소(새 IP, 다른 포트)로 다시 시작한 복제본은 새 주소를 자기 키로 서명해 리더에 알리고, 리더가 멤버십을 고칩니다. `ha remove`와 새 초대가 필요 없습니다.
- **밀어 주는 대기.** 작업의 `?wait=`, 새로 생긴 태스크 로그의 `?wait=`, `pumasi run`, `pumasi logs -f`가 작업이 끝나거나 출력이 오는 즉시 답합니다(CI에서 100ms 이내).
- **작업별 상한.** `pumasi quota alice --max-job-cpus 8 --max-job-memory 32G --max-job-cu 20`, 또는 `cluster.toml`의 `max_job_cpus`·`max_job_memory`·`max_job_cu`, 대시보드 계정 화면, API 정책 PATCH의 `max_job_*`. CPU·메모리를 넘는 태스크는 제출 때 거절하고, CU 상한을 다 쓴 작업은 아직 돌지 않은 태스크를 실패 처리합니다(이벤트 `job.cu_cap`, 돌던 태스크는 끝까지). 누가 제출하든 그 계정의 모든 작업에 적용됩니다.
- **`pumasi ui`의 ACME.** `--acme pumasi.example.com --acme-email ...`. 기본은 대시보드 포트의 TLS-ALPN-01, `--acme-challenge http-01`이면 80번 포트 리스너(나머지 요청은 HTTPS로 보냄). 계정 키와 인증서는 홈의 `acme/`에 두고, 만료 30일 전에 재시작 없이 갱신합니다. `--acme-directory letsencrypt-staging`, 사설 CA는 `--acme-directory URL --acme-ca 파일`.
- 에이전트 프로토콜 14(minor 10), 저장 레이아웃 7 그대로. 작업별 상한은 모든 복제본이 1.19여야 합니다.
- 알려진 한계: 충돌 시 마지막 2초의 제한 횟수는 잃을 수 있음, CU 상한 도달 시 실행 중이던 만큼 넘을 수 있음, DNS-01·와일드카드 없음, 새 주소로 재시작한 복제본이 선거를 한 번 일으킬 수 있음.
